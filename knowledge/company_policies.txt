SENTINEL TECHNOLOGIES - EMPLOYEE POLICIES

VACATION POLICY
Full-time employees receive 15 paid vacation days per year.
Vacation requests should be submitted at least 14 days in advance.

REMOTE WORK POLICY
Employees may work remotely up to three days per week.
Remote employees must be available during core business hours from
9:00 AM to 3:00 PM Pacific Time.

PASSWORD POLICY
Company passwords must contain at least 12 characters.
Passwords must include uppercase and lowercase letters, a number,
and a special character.

SECURITY INCIDENTS
Employees who suspect a security incident must immediately contact
the IT Security Team at security@sentinel.example.

EQUIPMENT POLICY
Company laptops and other equipment remain the property of
Sentinel Technologies and must be returned when employment ends.
